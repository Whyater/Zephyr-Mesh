Status levels

Every status is a shape, a color and a word together, so color is never the only signal.

LevelWordShapeColor tokenDarkLight
ok"OK"circleokOKOK
caution"Late"trianglecautionLateLate
alert"Lost" or "Inactive"squarealertLostLost
waiting"Waiting"hollow circletextSecondaryWaitingWaiting
unavailable"Not recorded"dashtextSecondaryNot recordedNot recorded
info"Info"infoinfoInfoInfo

Display thresholds: target age of 0.15 s or more reads Late; 0.3 s or more reads Lost. Display choice, not a safety limit.

App icon

A gust from the west wraps into an orbit of five drones linked to the point they track together. At 16 and 32 px the links and the second breeze are left out. The 128 and 64 px samples are scaled from ZephyrMesh-256.png; the 32 and 16 px samples are the real small frames from ZephyrMesh.ico.

Values on this page come from the token files. Contrast ratios are computed, not measured on a screen.
